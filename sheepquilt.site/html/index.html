Welcome to sheepquilt! This is a site I've been considering making for a long time as a way to patch my thoughts, projects, and interests together into a quilt-like collection.

What is this place?

To be entirely honest, I'm not really sure! My intention is that this will be a site that will grow with me. As I come up with new ideas and learn about new things, I will try to add them to this site somehow as a way to snapshot my progress through what will hopefully be years.

Why have I made this?

Mostly because I feel kind of lost in life right now (right now being 2026) and I'm hoping that by letting myself see my own work and progress that I'll have a better idea of where I'm going.

And of course so I can maybe get a job and not starve lol

Who am I?

As of the summer of 2026, I am a student at UCSD. I like playing video games, working out, cooking, and most of all, talking to people. Hopefully one day I can be a technical consultant, communicating with people about their systems and trading information on how to make things better in this world. There's much more about me through the link in the navigation bar, so if you'd like to know me better, feel free to explore there!
